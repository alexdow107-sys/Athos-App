Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Athos ("we", "us") is a fitness tracking and social app. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

Account info (email, username, display name), your training data (workouts, cardio, body stats), social content (posts, photos, comments, follows), and direct messages.

How we use it

To run the app: show your feed and profile, track your progress, deliver messages and notifications, and keep the community safe (handling reports and blocks).

What we share

We do not sell your personal data. Content you post publicly is visible to other users according to your privacy settings. Service providers (such as our database host) process data on our behalf to operate the app.

Your controls

You can edit your profile, set your account private, hide your followers, block users, and delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. Deleting your account removes your account and associated data.

Security

We protect your data with industry-standard measures, including password hashing and encrypted connections. No method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Athos is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
